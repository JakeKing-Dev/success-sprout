Cost

Success Sprout is free while it's in beta. Nobody will be charged for anything without agreeing to it first, and any change would be posted here well in advance. Whatever happens, your logs never lock: everything you've logged keeps working and stays on your device.

Ukiah Unified School District. The maker of Success Sprout works for Ukiah Unified. Success Sprout is free for Ukiah Unified, in full and permanently, for every school, classroom and staff member. The maker will not seek any payment, license fee or contract from Ukiah Unified for Success Sprout.

Your records

  • Everything you log is stored in your browser on your device. It isn't sent to Success Sprout or anyone else, and the maker can't see it.
  • Your records belong to you and your school. Your school decides how they're kept, shared and removed, and how long they're kept.
  • Because records exist only on your device, you're responsible for backups. Clearing the browser's data, losing the device or uninstalling the browser can erase them. Success Sprout reminds you to save backups; please do.

Using it at school

  • Check your school's rules before logging real students, and follow them, including rules about devices, student records and privacy.
  • Use initials or first names, as the app asks. Don't enter diagnoses, disability categories or full IEP text.
  • Success Sprout is a record-keeping tool. It doesn't give medical, behavioral or legal advice. Staff follow each student's plans (seizure action plans, behavior plans, IEPs) and district procedures, and decisions belong to the student's team.
